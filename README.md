> <br>— Series Note

**Arithmetic Spectral Descent** — *Obstruction Theory, Hodge–Krein Realization, and a Falsifiable Architecture for
the Riemann Hypothesis* — is a monograph in four volumes bound as one. It is the third work in the WiseMotif
Monograph of Mathematical Intelligence and belongs to the Grothendieckian Programme as a self-contained system: one of
the Programme's research subjects, a problem on which its method of recursive mathematical discovery is carried
through in full. A proposed structure is tested; what survives is abstracted and the question is posed again, in the
manner of Grothendieck's rising sea.

The problem is the Riemann Hypothesis, and the question is whether **local arithmetic can descend to a spectral
object that bears the zeros of the zeta function and controls where they lie.**

**The Riemann Hypothesis remains open. This work does not claim a proof of it, of the generalized Riemann
hypotheses, or of any equivalent statement.** It records a programme aimed at the conjecture, the tests to which that
programme was subjected, the parts that failed, the theorems obtained from those failures, and the construction
problems that remain once every failure has been accounted for.

## The idea: an index, not a positivity

Almost every natural mechanism that one might use to locate the zeros either uses information that does not
distinguish ζ from functions whose zeros lie off the line, or discards, at some step, exactly the information that
would be needed at the line. A proof must keep that information from the first step to the last. **The book is,
before anything else, a study of where it is lost.**

The title names the movement it examines. *Spectral*: the hope, from Hilbert and Pólya onward, that the zeros are
the spectrum of an operator. *Arithmetic*: the operator must come from the arithmetic of ℤ, not be written down after
the zeros are known. *Descent*: the passage from local to global — whether the local data descend to a single
arithmetic object carrying the zeros as its spectrum, together with a form that controls where that spectrum can lie.

The single substantive choice of the book is **the replacement of a positivity condition by an index condition.**
Definiteness proves too much (a positive polarization already forces the critical line and normality), it is
incompatible with the pole, and it has margins where the problem has none: Rodgers and Tao proved Λ ≥ 0, so the
Riemann Hypothesis is the equality Λ = 0. An index is an integer; it cannot be nearly right.

| The method | What the book does |
|---|---|
| **Bar** | proves, before any construction, where specified mechanisms lose the information needed at the critical line |
| **Test** | runs every mechanism on a firewall of four published counter-models: Davenport–Heilbronn, Beurling, Selberg, Epstein |
| **Decompose** | splits Weil's function-field proof into its actual inputs, so that each can be sought over ℤ separately |
| **Audit** | measures what the sphere spectrum, cyclotomic homology and prismatic cohomology supply, and what they do not |
| **Reduce** | proves the conditional chain from an index-one Hodge–Krein realization to the Riemann Hypothesis |
| **Certify** | separates certified finite computation from asymptotic claims; publishes the scripts and data |
| **Pose** | states the two remaining obligations precisely enough to be attacked, rejected or falsified |

## Selected results

- **Krein Index Theorem (Theorem D).** If a nondegenerate Hermitian form *G* of inertia (n₊, n₋) and an operator Θ
  satisfy Θ\*G + GΘ = G, then the number of eigenvalues of Θ with Re λ > ½, counted with algebraic multiplicity, is
  at most min(n₊, n₋).
- **Index-One Exhaustion Theorem.** With stable spectral exhaustion, centered duality and compatible negative index
  at most one, AHK-1 ∧ AHK-2 ∧ AHK-3 ⟹ RH — a proved conditional implication. The equivalence audit shows why the
  three hypotheses are not by themselves weaker than the conclusion: arithmetic content must enter through AHK-4 to AHK-6.
- **Amplification Invariance Theorem.** For Dirichlet series whose singular support contains *k*-fold sums of zeros,
  σ_c − Re(amplified singularity) = k(½ − δ) identically, independently of normalization: amplification alone gains
  nothing in the zero exponent.
- **Support doubling, corrected.** The counterfactual zero signal grows like e^{δS} while the absolute arithmetic
  envelope grows like S e^{S/2}, so the threshold is δ > ½, not the spurious δ > ¼ obtained by confusing supp g with
  supp(g ∗ g̃).
- **Criticality.** Since Rodgers–Tao proved Λ ≥ 0, a perturbation-stable strict positivity statement, a uniform
  spectral gap or a finite strict sum-of-squares certificate cannot by itself prove RH.
- **The generalized hypotheses.** For the extended Selberg class, AHK-1_L–AHK-3_L hold if and only if *F* has at most
  one nontrivial zero with Re ρ > ½; the residual gap is exactly the Landau–Siegel problem.
- **Finite evidence, kept as such.** Sixty-three pole-removed Guinand–Weil Gram matrices have exactly one measured
  negative direction in every configuration; the measurement is finite evidence and cannot be inserted into the
  conditional theorem.

## What this work is — and is not

**It is** a work of obstruction theory and of precise reduction: where specified proof mechanisms fail, a proved
conditional chain, a certified computation stack, and two obligations stated precisely enough to be attacked,
rejected or falsified — the Arithmetic Hodge–Krein Realization Problem and Problem A2⋆. Every claim carries its
status where it is made: **[T]** theorem, **[C]** conditional, **[O]** open construction, **[E]** evidence,
**[N]** proved impossibility within stated hypotheses, **[K]** known background.

**It is not** a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. No system satisfying AHK-1 to AHK-3 is constructed in this work. The
no-go theorems exclude specified classes of mechanisms, not all possible proofs. A failed test can end a branch of the
programme; no number of passed finite tests can establish one.

## Contributing

A programme that cannot fail is not a mathematical programme, and this one is written to make failure easy to
report. Three kinds of contribution matter most:

- **Refutation** — a counterexample to a theorem, a mechanism the firewall should have caught, or a run of one of the
  falsification tests F1–F10 that cuts an edge of the dependency graph.
- **Construction** — progress on the Arithmetic Hodge–Krein Realization Problem or on Problem A2⋆; a construction
  satisfying AHK-1 and AHK-2 but not AHK-3 would already be progress.
- **Reproduction** — an independent run of the numerics, with interval arithmetic where the book reports floating
  point.

Report errors through an [errata issue](https://github.com/AlienMuse/arithmetic-spectral-descent/issues/new?template=errata.yml). Accepted corrections are
acknowledged by name in the next edition (see [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md)).
